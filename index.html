Methodology & coverage

Statistics are based on the supplied event leaderboard snapshot. Nation #1444 alliance percentages represent each alliance's share of points among Nation #1444 players appearing in the captured leaderboard. They are not necessarily the total points of every member of an alliance if players fall outside the captured ranking.

Nation #1444 ranks are calculated by points, descending, after filtering to #1444. Ties, if present, are ordered by overall event rank. Percentages use full precision internally and are displayed to two decimal places; rounded shares may not sum to exactly 100%. Averages are displayed to two decimal places.
